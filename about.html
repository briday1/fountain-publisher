FOUNTAIN PUBLISHER

A place to write your screenplay.

Fountain Publisher is a browser-based screenplay editor. Write on the formatted page, organize scenes and beats, explore character dialogue and story pacing, and export a screenplay PDF. Your writing saves as a portable Fountain file.

Open Fountain Publisher

Your writing, on your device

You can write, import files, and export your work without connecting an account. The browser keeps a local workspace on your device. Download a Fountain file to keep a separate copy.

Optional Google Drive and GitHub connections

Connect Google Drive to browse folders, search for screenplays, open existing files, and save your changes. Shared Drive screenplays support live collaboration with people who have access to the file. Google account details identify you to your collaborators; Drive permissions determine who can read or edit.

The Drive connection requests permission to view and manage Drive files so its file browser can navigate existing folders and screenplays. Account connections and live document synchronization are processed through Cloudflare. The privacy policy explains what information is handled and how to request deletion.

You can also connect GitHub to browse repositories and save Fountain files. Connecting either service is optional.
