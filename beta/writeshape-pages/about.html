About WriteShape

WriteShape is a browser editor for screenplays and books, made by Brian Day. Screenplays save as Fountain files; books save as Markdown. You can keep working with those files outside WriteShape.

Open WriteShape

Writing and saving

Write, open local files, and export a standard PDF without an account. Your browser keeps a draft on this device. Download a copy to keep your work somewhere else, too.

Premium

Premium adds the cloud library, Google Drive saving, writing goals, story insights, a beat sheet and beat guide, phone-sized screenplay PDFs, and character highlighting. Live editing lets people with access work on the same cloud or Drive document.

If Premium ends, your saved cloud files remain available to read and download. Make a local copy to keep editing. New cloud saves and live editing require Premium.

Private testing

WriteShape currently requires approved access. Premium is included for approved private testers. Payment screens use test billing; no real charges are enabled.

Help

Use Report a problem in the app, or email support@writeshape.com. Tell us what you were trying to do and what went wrong. Please leave private writing out of reports and screenshots.
