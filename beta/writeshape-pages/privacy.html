WRITESHAPE

Privacy Policy

Updated October 1, 2026

WriteShape is operated by Brian Day. This page explains where your writing is stored, what account information the app uses, and what happens when you connect a service or contact support.

Writing on your device

Drafts, recovery copies, preferences, writing goals, and pending changes are stored in your browser. Cloud and Drive documents can also leave local copies so you can recover work or continue after a connection drops. Clearing the site's browser data can remove these copies.

If you choose a local folder, the browser asks for permission to read and save files there. Local files do not reach another device unless you copy them or save them to shared storage.

Cloud files and live editing

The WriteShape library stores document contents, filenames, folders, ownership, and saved versions on Cloudflare. Live editing also stores document changes on Cloudflare, including when the file itself is in Google Drive. Collaborators with access receive the document and can see each other's presence, names, and cursors.

WriteShape processes document contents to open, save, recover, export, and share your work. Cloud storage and live editing are not end-to-end encrypted: the service needs to process the text to provide those features. Removing someone's access stops future authorized access; it cannot take back copies they already received.

Accounts and Google Drive

Google sign-in gives WriteShape your Google account identifier, email address, and display name. The private access gate also uses your email to check whether you are an approved tester. Authentication cookies keep you signed in.

Connecting Google Drive is a separate choice. It requests permission to view and manage Drive files so you can browse existing folders and open and save documents. WriteShape processes the file metadata and contents needed for those actions, using your Google permissions. Connection credentials are encrypted before storage.

Disconnecting Drive in WriteShape removes its stored Drive credentials. You can also revoke access in your Google account. Neither action deletes files in Drive, browser recovery copies, or existing live-editing data stored by WriteShape.

Payments

Stripe provides payment screens and subscription management. WriteShape stores billing identifiers and plan status. Card details are entered into Stripe's screens. During private testing, billing uses test mode and no real charges are enabled.

Error reports and support

Application error logs contain an error reference, the type of request, and its status. That error handler leaves out document text, account identifiers, credentials, and URL query strings. Cloudflare and other service providers may keep their own operational logs.

Report a problem lets you review optional technical details before sending them: app version, browser, screen size, connection and sync status, and recent error references. Those details do not include your writing, filenames, or account email. The report opens in your email app; you choose whether to send it.

Support receives your email address, message, and any attachments you send. Cloudflare forwards support mail to an AgentMail inbox. AI tools may help review reports and prepare fixes. Please review screenshots and leave out private writing, passwords, and payment details.

Keeping and removing data

Cloud documents and saved versions currently have no automatic expiry. Moving a file to Trash keeps its contents and history so it can be restored. Signing out, disconnecting Drive, or ending Premium does not delete your documents or account. Local copies remain until you remove them or clear the site's browser data.

Account deletion is not available as a button in the app. To request access to or deletion of data held by WriteShape, email support@writeshape.com. We will confirm the account involved and explain what can be removed, including any copies that remain with service providers or on devices.

Changes and contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@writeshape.com. Changes to this page will carry a new date. This policy describes the current private preview; it will be updated before paid public access begins.
