Back up your cloud data

After Premium access ends, you have 30 days to back up your WriteShape cloud documents and saved version history. Canceling renewal does not shorten your paid access. During the backup window, cloud files are read-only.

The window ends at 11:59:59 p.m. Eastern Time on your deadline date. Expiration is at the following midnight, using America/New_York time, including daylight saving changes. See your exact deadline in Account or your reminder email.

Open WriteShape

Download files to your device

  1. Sign in to the WriteShape account that owns your files.
  2. Choose File → Files, then WriteShape cloud.
  3. Open each folder, select the files you want, and choose Download. Check Trash too if you want to keep those files.
  4. Keep the downloaded Fountain files for screenplays and Markdown files for books somewhere safe. Open a local copy to keep editing with Basic.

Keep previous versions

Current-file downloads do not include previous versions. Open Version history for each file, select versions you want to preserve, and download them separately. Premium retains the newest 50 previous versions; versions that have already expired cannot be recovered.

Copy files to Google Drive

  1. In your cloud library, select the files you want to keep.
  2. Choose Copy to Google Drive. Connect your Google account if prompted, then return to your library and select the files again.
  3. Your copies are saved to the root of your Google Drive. Open Drive and check the copies before your deadline.

Copying preserves the cloud originals. This backup option remains available during the grace period; ordinary new cloud saves, Drive editing, and live editing still require Premium. Download previous versions separately to preserve their history. If Drive is unavailable, download your files locally.

What happens at the deadline?

Your WriteShape cloud documents, folders, Trash, saved version history, and related cloud sharing data are automatically removed after the deadline. Your account, local files, and Google Drive files are preserved. Deleted cloud files cannot be restored by reactivating Premium afterward.

Keep Premium instead

Reactivate Premium before deletion to cancel the backup deadline and pending reminders. We email your account address when the backup window begins, with 10 days left, and with 1 day left. Reminder checks run in the Eastern morning.
