## Writing and collaboration

Your documents can contain text, formatting, metadata and saved history. Export options include Markdown and Book PDF, DOCX, EPUB and RTF; export layout can differ between formats and reading applications. Maintain a separate copy of important work. This description does not promise uninterrupted availability or a particular recovery period.

Library sharing permits specific existing accounts to read or edit a document. Google Drive sharing follows Google permissions. Removing access prevents future authorized participation but cannot recall copies already received. Concurrent direct-file edits may require resolving a conflict and preserving separate copies.
