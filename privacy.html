FOUNTAIN PUBLISHER

Privacy Policy

Effective September 7, 2026

Fountain Publisher is a screenplay editor that can connect to Google Drive and GitHub and provide live collaboration through Cloudflare. This policy explains what information the application handles and why.

Information we process

When you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account identifier, name, email address, profile image, OAuth access token, and, when Google provides one, a refresh token. When you connect GitHub, we receive the authorization tokens needed to perform the repository actions you request.

Screenplays normally remain in your browser. If you choose Google Drive, screenplay files remain in Google Drive and are governed by your Drive permissions. Live collaboration also stores an encoded synchronization snapshot in a Cloudflare Durable Object so collaborators can reconnect. The active room processes document changes, cursor positions, selections, display names, email addresses, access roles, and presence state. If you use GitHub features, the application processes the repositories, branches, paths, and file content needed for the action you select.

How information is used

Google user data is used only to sign you in, show files you select or create with Fountain Publisher, save your changes, manage sharing at your direction, verify access to collaboration rooms, and display collaborator identity and presence. GitHub data is used only for repository browsing and file operations you request. We do not sell personal information, use document content for advertising, or train machine-learning models on it.

Storage and security

OAuth tokens are stored server-side in Cloudflare D1 and encrypted using AES-GCM. Authentication cookies are Secure and HttpOnly. Google Drive remains the durable file store. Cloudflare processes authentication, encrypted credentials, rate limits, and collaboration snapshots. Google and GitHub process information under their own policies.

Sharing

Drive sharing is controlled by Google Drive permissions. People you invite can access the file according to the role you choose. During a live session, authorized collaborators receive document updates and the identity and cursor presence of other participants.

Retention and control

Application sessions expire after 30 days unless renewed. Signing out removes the corresponding server session. You can revoke Fountain Publisher from your Google or GitHub account settings and manage or delete screenplay files and sharing permissions in Google Drive. Collaboration snapshots may remain on Cloudflare for continuity; to request deletion of application-held account or collaboration data, contact us with the relevant account and file details.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Fountain Publisher’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the application changes. Material revisions will be reflected by the effective date above. Questions or deletion requests can be sent to brian.day1@gmail.com.
