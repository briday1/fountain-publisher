FOUNTAIN PUBLISHER

Privacy Policy

Effective September 19, 2026

Fountain Publisher is a screenplay editor that can connect to Google Drive and GitHub and provide live collaboration through Cloudflare. This policy explains what information the application handles and why.

Information we process

When you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account identifier, name, email address, profile image, OAuth access token, and, when Google provides one, a refresh token. When you connect GitHub, we receive the authorization tokens needed to perform the repository actions you request.

The Google Drive connection requests permission to view and manage Drive files. When you browse or search Drive, the application processes your search query and file, folder, and shared-drive metadata, such as names, identifiers, file types, modification times, and access capabilities. This lets you navigate existing folders and find screenplays, including files that were not created with Fountain Publisher. When you choose a screenplay to open, save, share, or view its history, the application processes the file content, revisions, or permissions needed for that action.

Local writing and recovery copies are stored in your browser. When you use Google Drive, files are saved in Drive and remain subject to Drive permissions. Live collaboration also stores screenplay synchronization data in Cloudflare Durable Objects and an account-specific cache on your device so collaborators can reconnect. Active rooms process document changes, cursor positions, selections, display names, email addresses, access roles, and presence state. If you use GitHub features, the application processes the repositories, branches, paths, and file content needed for the action you select.

How information is used

Google user data is used only to sign you in, browse and search Drive for screenplays and folders, open the files you choose, save your changes, manage sharing at your direction, verify access to collaboration rooms, and display collaborator identity and presence. GitHub data is used only for repository browsing and file operations you request. We do not sell personal information, use document content for advertising, or train machine-learning models on it.

Storage and security

OAuth tokens are stored server-side in Cloudflare D1 and encrypted using AES-GCM. Authentication cookies are Secure and HttpOnly. Cloudflare processes authentication, encrypted credentials, rate limits, Drive file operations, and collaboration data. Live collaboration is not end-to-end encrypted: the service processes screenplay contents to synchronize and save changes. Google and GitHub process information under their own policies.

Sharing

Drive sharing is controlled by Google Drive permissions. People you invite can access the file according to the role you choose. During a live session, authorized collaborators receive document updates and the identity and cursor presence of other participants.

Retention and control

Application sessions expire after 30 days unless renewed. Signing out removes the corresponding server session; it does not erase your local drafts, files in Drive or GitHub, or collaboration data. You can revoke Fountain Publisher from your Google or GitHub account settings and manage or delete screenplay files and sharing permissions in those services. Local workspace and recovery data remain on your device until you remove them or clear the site's browser storage. Collaboration data may remain on Cloudflare for continuity; to request deletion of application-held account or collaboration data, contact us with the relevant account and file details.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Fountain Publisher’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the application changes. Material revisions will be reflected by the effective date above. Questions or deletion requests can be sent to brian.day1@gmail.com.
